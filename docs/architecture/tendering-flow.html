Invariants that hold at every step
Rule 2 · Grounding
llm_reasoning.ask() drops any AI output citing a document/entity not sent to it. Every persisted requirement/finding carries source doc · page · clause · confidence — required at the schema layer, not optional.
Rule 4 · Tenant isolation
Every route through _load_case_or_403 (org/role scoped). Vector search is case_id-scoped; evidence matching adds org_id scope so one supplier's vault is never searched for another. Storage, logs, background jobs inherit the caller's scope.
Rule 3 · Human-in-the-loop
AI records land pending. No endpoint auto-confirms compliance, approves evidence, or makes the bid decision — those writes are always user-initiated and audit-logged.
